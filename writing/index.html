    常見錯誤

    原句問題修改
    Many people discussed that whether…whether 前不加 that，現象用現在式Many people discuss whether…
    …and other think…other 當名詞要用複數…and others think…
    it's not important正式寫作避免縮寫，且沒對上題目的「required」it is not necessary to make these courses compulsory
    （開頭只並列兩方說法）Opinion 題缺少立場，會影響 Task Response結尾加上 In my view, …
